Strip hidden data from photos and videos: C2PA credentials, EXIF, GPS, XMP, IPTC and AI prompt text. Files are processed in this page and never uploaded.

What this does: photos are cleaned without re-compressing (pixels stay identical). In MP4/MOV videos the metadata is overwritten with blank space, so the file size stays the same and the video is not re-encoded.
What it can't do: remove invisible watermarks or change the picture itself, and some phone videos keep a separate GPS/sensor track that needs re-encoding to remove (the tool warns you when it finds one). HEIC files must be converted to JPG first.
